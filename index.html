Agentic RL on AReaL 2.0

Aug 2026 – Present

Undergraduate Research, supervised by Prof. Binhang YUAN

HKUST CSE

  • Integrated a Code-as-World-style executable-world discovery agent into AReaL 2.0's agent-service architecture (external-model, online mode). An LLM writes a physical world specification, which is compiled to MuJoCo, simulated, and checked against observations, with up to 5 rounds of residual feedback to revise it.
  • Built the rollout driver, the session/reward interface (binary verifier reward), and a turn-by-turn parity check against AReaL-stored trajectories. In a Qwen3-Coder pilot, 30/30 trajectories passed the parity check, and multi-turn revision raised task success from 7% to 37%.
  • Clarified Ray scheduler support in the agent workflow compatibility notes (#1567). See my contributions.
